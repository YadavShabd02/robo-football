What you learn here: how minimax and α-β actually search a real robo-football position: α/β windows flowing down, values flowing up, and exactly where and why branches are cut. Hover any node to see its board.

The real tree is huge (≈6–13 moves with the ball, ≈20–34 without), so only the first k moves per node are drawn (best-first if ordering is on). Leaves are scored by the heuristic from the root player's view; the summary on the right also reports the cost of searching the full tree.
